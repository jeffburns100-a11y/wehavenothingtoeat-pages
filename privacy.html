Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

We Have Nothing To Eat ("the app") helps you track food inventory, scan barcodes, and get recipe suggestions. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Account information

When you sign up, we collect your email address and a password to create your account. Authentication is handled by Supabase; we do not store your password ourselves.

Inventory and recipe data

Food items you add, barcodes you scan, shopping list entries, dietary preferences, and recipes you generate or cook are stored so the app can show them back to you. This data is private to your account and is not shared with other users.

Camera and photos

If you use the photo or video scanning features, images are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to identify food items. Images are used only to generate that identification and are not stored by us after the request completes.

Barcode lookups

Barcodes you scan are looked up against the free, public Open Food Facts database to fetch product details. This lookup result may be cached to speed up future scans of the same product.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Data deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email address below.
